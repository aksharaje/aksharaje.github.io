thinking about appropriate reliance

Hi, I'm Akshara, a product manager with an MBA. I spend a lot of my time thinking about how people actually come to trust (and sometimes adapt) the AI tools they use at work. The questions I care about came from the job: watching the gap between what a model does and what the person using it assumes about it, and realizing nobody really measures that gap. I'm exploring PhD opportunities starting Fall 2027 in human–AI interaction, with a particular interest in appropriate reliance and end-user customization of AI.

  2. Position: Accountability Mechanisms Are Adopted Only When the Verifier Pays Nothing
    Akshara Raje · preprint, 2026 · pdf
    A hand-coded census of 2,942 freelance job postings finds near-zero demand for AI disclosure, watermarking, or provenance, and argues the pattern follows from cost incidence: accountability is adopted only when the verifier doesn't pay for it.

    Mechanisms whose cost falls on the applicant are routinely requested; those whose cost falls on the verifier are not.
